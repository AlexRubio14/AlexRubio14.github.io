// ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Alex!

I'm a Game Software Engineer focused on C++ systems, multiplayer networking and gameplay programming.

I enjoy understanding how things work under the hood, especially when the problem is complex. I like designing systems from the ground up, figuring out how their pieces should interact, and finding ways to keep them reliable and maintainable as they grow.

My experience combines professional gameplay programming at The Breach Studios with personal work in Unreal Engine, C++ and multiplayer networking. Through these projects, I've worked on everything from gameplay systems and character controllers to low-level networking, serialization, prediction and automated testing.

I'm naturally curious and tend to dig deeper whenever I encounter something I don't understand. That's what led me to build NetworkMiddleware from scratch: to explore multiplayer networking beyond the engine level and understand the systems underneath it.

I'm looking to keep working on technically challenging problems, learn from experienced engineers, and build game technology that I'm genuinely proud of.
