// ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Alex!

I'm a software engineer focused on networking and the systems behind multiplayer games. I enjoy understanding how things work under the hood, especially when the problem is complex. C++, low level networking, system design and optimization are the areas I enjoy most, and I love the feeling of making a difficult system finally click into place. I'm naturally curious, so if I come across something I don't understand, I'll probably end up researching it just because I want to know how it works.

I've developed this mindset through personal projects and professional studio experience at The Breach Studios. My final year project, Lyra, was a year long project where I worked as Gameplay Programmer & Producer in a team of 7 people, winning the Best TFG award at ENTI's 2026 DemoDay. I later built NetworkMiddleware from scratch to push myself further into C++ networking. I'm ambitious about where I want to take my career. I want to work on challenging systems, keep learning from the people around me, and eventually build networking and multiplayer technology that I'm genuinely proud of.
