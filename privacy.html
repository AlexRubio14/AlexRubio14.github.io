Privacy

What happens to your data on this site. The short version: no cookies, no tracking, and your email is only used to reply to you.

Contact form

When you send a message, the form collects your email address, the subject, the message and the site language. I, Alejandro Rubio Plana (alexrubioplana14@gmail.com), am responsible for this data and I only use it to reply to you.

Legal basis: your consent, given when you send the message (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by asking me to delete your message.

How it travels: the form goes to a small server of mine on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards it to my inbox through Resend, an email delivery service. That server does not store messages. Cloudflare also processes your IP address to block spam (a limit of 3 messages per minute and its Turnstile check).

Outside the EU: Cloudflare and Resend are US companies, so the data may leave the European Union. Both offer the safeguards the GDPR requires for these transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long: I keep the message in my inbox while we are in touch, and I delete it if you ask me to.

The rest of the site

  • No cookies and no analytics. That is why there is no cookie banner.
  • The site only saves your language choice in your browser (localStorage). It never leaves your device.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you visit, to serve the pages and keep the service secure.
  • Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play. From then on, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address.
  • Fonts are served from this site itself, not from external services.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, or to restrict or object to its use, by writing to alexrubioplana14@gmail.com. If you think I have not handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, aepd.es ).
